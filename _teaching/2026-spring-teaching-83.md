The Michigander Scholars Program, funded by the Michigan Economic Development Corporation (MEDC) and the TAT (Talent Action Team), aims to enhance Michigan's talent pool in the semiconductor sector. The University of Michigan offers this program to students pursuing degrees in computer, electrical, process, or software engineering. The main goal of this program is to support students who are pursuing careers in the broader semiconductor domain, and are planning to launch their career within the Michigan state.
