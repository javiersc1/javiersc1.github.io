The SolidWorks Associate Certification, or CSWA, is an introductory certification that demonstrates a user's foundational skills in using SolidWorks, a popular 3D CAD design software. It assesses proficiency in creating parts, assemblies, and drawings, as well as understanding design concepts and engineering principles. This certification is ideal for students and new professionals in engineering, design, and manufacturing fields, serving as a credential that validates their capability to efficiently use SolidWorks in real-world applications.
