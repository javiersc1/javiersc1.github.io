The LPI Linux Essentials certification is an entry-level certification offered by the Linux Professional Institute. It is designed for individuals who are new to Linux and want to gain foundational knowledge and skills in Linux operating systems. The certification covers essential topics such as the basic concepts of open source, the basics of the Linux command line, understanding files and scripts, and security and permissions. It serves as a stepping stone for more advanced Linux certifications and is ideal for anyone looking to understand and use Linux in various environments.
