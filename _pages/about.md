Hi! My name is Javier (pronounced like ha-vee-air). I am an algorithms engineer at KLA, a semiconductor inspection company partially located in Ann Arbor. I am creating machine learning and image/signal processing algorithms for semiconductor defect inspection to support fab foundries in process node developments. Before that, I was a PhD Candidate in the Signal and Image Processing & Machine Learning (SIPML) track of the [Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE)](https://ece.engin.umich.edu/) department at the University of Michigan. I was co-advised by [Laura Balzano](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~girasole/), [Jeffrey Fessler](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~fessler/), and [Scott Peltier](https://fmri.research.umich.edu/about/faculty/peltier.php).
